# Prompt-and-Diff Log

## AI Use

I did not use AI to generate my original ADR. I wrote the ADR myself based on the architecture and design decisions I made for my Food Waste Reducer application.

Because I did not use an AI-generated version, I do not have an AI prompt-and-diff comparison for the original ADR.

## My Drafting Process

I first identified a real architecture decision that I made for my application: using a single Python modular application with SQLite for data persistence.

I then wrote the ADR using the required sections:

- Context
- The Decision
- Alternatives Considered
- Consequences

I considered different ways of storing data, including in-memory storage, JSON files, a hosted database, and microservices. I explained why I chose SQLite and why I did not choose the other options.

## Changes I Made

After writing the first draft, I reviewed the ADR to make sure:

- The decision was clearly stated.
- The alternatives were realistic options I could have chosen.
- The consequences explained both benefits and drawbacks.
- The wording was clear and student-friendly.
- The ADR matched my actual application.

I made wording and organization changes during my review, but I did not use an AI-generated ADR as the original version.

## Final Result

The final ADR represents my own architecture decision and explains the trade-offs of using a Python modular application with SQLite.
