# Food Waste Reducer
## Concept Brief
Food Waste Reducer is a smart grocery management app that helps people use the food they already have before it goes to waste. Users can track food in their fridge and pantry, receive reminders about items that should be used soon, and discover recipes based on ingredients they already own. Users can add groceries manually or scan a receipt to automatically build their pantry. The app prioritizes food that is approaching its expiration date and recommends recipes that use those ingredients first. It can also create a shopping list based on missing items and provide a dashboard showing how much food and money users have saved. The goal is to help people save money, waste less food, and make meal planning easier.
